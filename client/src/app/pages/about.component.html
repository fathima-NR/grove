Our grove

A small market with a loud color palette.

Grove is a modern organic market: citrus that still smells like the tree, berries that do not travel well unless they leave today, and juice pressed before breakfast.

Fruit, nuts, and cold-pressed bottles. If it needs a long ingredient list, it does not make the crate.

How it ships

Delivery is $5.95, and free once the basket reaches $40. Glass comes back if you want it to.

Where it grows

Coastal orchards, upland berry fields, and a press that starts before the city is awake.
